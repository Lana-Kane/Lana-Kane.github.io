Services

Landscape design, from the first idea to the final plant.

Everything on this page is something I do myself. When you work with me, you work with me directly, from our first conversation to the last plant going in the ground.

01

Garden Design

I design your whole garden around the way you live. I visit the space, take my time to understand it, and draw up a design that works beautifully and feels right to be in. Each design is made for you, shaped by how you use the space and how much upkeep you want.

Ideal for new gardens, complete redesigns, and gardens that no longer suit how you live.

02

Planting Plans

I choose every plant for your soil, the light in your garden and the amount of care you want to give. Then I plant it myself, so the garden is put in exactly as I designed it. I love getting the right plant in the right place, and it makes all the difference to how a garden grows.

Ideal for borders and beds, and gardens that should look good through every season.

03

Drip Irrigation

I plan and install drip irrigation systems that water your garden slowly, at the roots, exactly where it is needed. I design the layout around your planting, fit everything myself and set it up so your garden is looked after with very little effort from you. It is one of the most useful things you can add to a garden as our summers get hotter.

Ideal for borders, raised beds, containers, new planting, and anyone who wants a garden that copes with hot, dry summers.

04

Hardscaping

The paths, patios and other hard surfaces and structures that give a garden its shape. I carry out this work myself, so the finish matches the design and the garden feels like one complete space rather than separate parts.

Ideal for gardens that need structure, somewhere to sit, or a better way to move through the space.

05

Consultations

A visit to your garden to talk through what you want, what is not working and where to begin. You might arrive with a clear vision and need help making it happen, or simply want a space that feels good and have no idea where to start. Either is a perfect place to begin.

Ideal for anyone who wants honest, practical advice before deciding on a bigger project.

Why drip irrigation

Better for your garden and for water

A well planned drip system gives your plants what they need and wastes almost nothing.

Uses far less water

Water is delivered slowly at the roots, so very little is lost to evaporation, runoff or spray. Less water is wasted on leaves, paths and weeds.

Made for hotter summers

With UK summers getting hotter and drier, using water carefully matters more every year. Drip irrigation keeps your plants thriving while using only what they need.

Works during a hosepipe ban

Drip irrigation is generally permitted during a hosepipe ban when it is installed to meet water company requirements, so your garden keeps its water in a drought. The rules vary by supplier, so I design your system around yours.

Healthier plants

Slow, steady watering at the roots encourages deep, strong growth and keeps the leaves dry, which helps plants cope with heat and stay healthier.

Saves you time

A system can be fitted with a timer, so your garden is watered automatically, even when you are busy or away.

Lower water use, lower bills

Using less water is better for the environment, and if you are on a water meter it can reduce your bill too.
