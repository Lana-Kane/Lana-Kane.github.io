Services

Landscape design, from the first idea to the final plant.

From our first conversation to the last plant going in the ground, I can help you handle it all. Everything on this page comes as part of the service, so you never need to hire anyone else.

Garden Design

I design your whole garden around the way you live. I visit the space, take my time to understand it, and draw up a design that works beautifully and feels right to be in, shaped by how you use it and how much upkeep you want.

Ideal for new gardens, complete redesigns, and gardens that no longer suit how you live.

Planting Plans

I choose every plant for your soil, the light in your garden and the amount of care you want to give. The planting is then taken care of for you, so the garden goes in exactly as designed.

Ideal for borders and beds, and gardens that should look good through every season.

Drip Irrigation

Drip irrigation planned and installed for you, watering your plants slowly at the roots and using far less water than a hosepipe. That matters as our summers get hotter, and drip systems are generally permitted during hosepipe bans.

Ideal for borders, raised beds, containers and new planting.

Hardscaping

The paths, patios and other hard surfaces and structures that give a garden its shape. It is all taken care of for you, so the finish matches the design and the whole garden feels like one space.

Ideal for gardens that need structure, somewhere to sit, or a better way to move through the space.

Raised Beds and Growing Spaces

Raised vegetable beds, greenhouses and productive growing areas, designed to look beautiful and be a pleasure to use. I can help you plan the whole space, and it is all built and planted for you.

Ideal for anyone who wants to grow their own food, herbs and flowers without it taking over the garden.

Consultations

A visit to your garden to talk through what you want, what is not working and where to begin. Whether you have a clear vision or no idea where to start, it is the perfect first step.

Ideal for anyone who wants honest, practical advice before deciding on a bigger project.

Made around you

No two gardens are the same

The way you use a space, and how much looking after you are happy to do, shape every decision I make.

Low maintenance

If you want a garden that looks wonderful without taking over your weekends, I will design and plant it that way from the start.

Plenty to enjoy

If you love to potter, I will design a garden with borders and planting that give you plenty to tend and enjoy.

Not sure which service you need?

That is completely fine. Get in touch and I will talk it through with you.
