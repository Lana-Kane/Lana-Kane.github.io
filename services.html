Services

Landscape design, from first sketch
to finished planting.

Each service can stand alone or build into a full project — most gardens move through several of these stages.

Design

Garden Design

A complete concept for the space — layout, structure, mood and materials — worked out on-site and drawn up so you can see exactly how the garden will function before anything is built. Suited to full redesigns, new builds, and gardens that no longer serve how you live.

Planting

Planting Plans

A detailed plant palette matched to your soil, aspect and upkeep preferences — from low-maintenance structural planting to a full seasonal border. Delivered as a plan you or a contractor can plant directly from.

Structure

Hardscaping

Paths, patios, steps, walling, raised beds and pergolas — the built elements that give a garden its structure and make it usable in every season. Specified and detailed for you or a chosen contractor to build from.

Advice

Consultations

A focused on-site visit for when you need direction, not a full design — working out what's going wrong, what to prioritise, and where to start. Often the first step before a larger project.

Not sure which you need?

Most projects start with a consultation — get in touch and we'll talk it through.
