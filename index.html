What we do

Four ways to begin

Every garden starts somewhere different — a blank plot, a tired lawn, or a single idea that needs shaping.

i.

Garden Design

Full design concepts that balance how a garden looks with how you'll actually use it, room by room, season by season.

ii.

Planting Plans

Considered plant selections suited to your soil, light and maintenance appetite — built to look good long after installation.

iii.

Hardscaping

Paths, patios, walling and structure — the bones that hold a design together through every season.

iv.

Consultations

An on-site session for honest, practical advice when you need direction rather than a full design.

Based in Hertfordshire

Designed on-site,

Lana Kane Designs works directly with the site — its light, its soil, its existing planting — rather than starting from a fixed idea. Every project is walked, measured and discussed in person before a single line is drawn.
