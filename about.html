About the studio

Landscape design led by the site itself.

Lana Kane Designs is a landscape design studio working across London, Hertfordshire and the surrounding areas. Every project begins the same way — on-site, on foot, working out how a garden is actually used before deciding how it should look.

That approach shapes everything that follows: planting chosen for the soil that's actually there, structure built for the way a family moves through the space, and designs that are meant to be lived in rather than just photographed.
